# Season Practice Outlines: MSB 341 Product Management (Fall 2026)

The season skeleton: one outline per practice, in order. Each gives the axis, the emphasis, the opener (a scripture and a quote tied to that emphasis, per the Wooden/Maeser model), the coach's story slot, the season ritual that runs, and the segment focus.

This is the pre-season plan, not the finished plan. Write the full minute-by-minute the week of, from `practice-plan-template.md`. Pull the coach's story from `story-catalog.md`. Season benchmarks (sprints, exams, peer work) are the source of truth in `00-schedule.qmd`; if a date here disagrees with the schedule, the schedule wins.

The course runs on six builder axes, two weeks each (Agentic Workflow, Discovery, Design, Application Architecture, AI Systems, Launch and Learn), and six two-week sprints. There are no weekly quizzes; own understanding is measured by the midterm (Oct 20 to 22) and final (Dec 8 to 9) in the testing center.

The openers below extend the truth-seeking opener bank in `practice-plan-template.md` across the whole season. The thread is product management as truth-seeking: Aim well, get evidence, believe in proportion to it, and act. Swap any pairing for one that fits the room that week.

**Sprint days.** Every other practice (1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11) opens a sprint. The previous sprint's review, retro, and 60 to 90 second Loom demo are due that day, and students run `/sprint-plan` in class. The plan is due on Canvas that Wednesday at 11:59 PM, graded for completion, and students may change it during the sprint as long as they note what changed.

Every practice ends with the huddle: two or three students show their deploy URL to the room. Small, frequent, low-stakes exposure to each other's work is the main peer-network mechanism, and it feeds the demo rounds.

## Practice 3 · Mon, Sep 28 · Discovery

- **Sprint / phase:** Sprint day. Sprint 1 due, Sprint 2 assigned.
- **Emphasis:** What to build, and whether you should. Strategy is choosing what not to do, and a winning product can still leave users worse off.
- **Scripture:** Matthew 7:20, "by their fruits ye shall know them." Judge a product by its effect on the people who use it.
- **Quote:** "The essence of strategy is choosing what not to do." (Michael Porter) Alternate: "Make something people want." (Paul Graham, Y Combinator)
- **Coach's story:** a time a good outcome hid a bad decision, or a product that worked but should not have shipped.
- **Season ritual:** huddle. Sprint 2 plans committed in class.
- **Film:** two Sprint 1 plan files, one specific and testable, one vague.
- **Install:** where to play and how to win, creating and capturing value, five forces, blue oceans, moats, the DHM test, product morality (virtue and the golden mean).
- **Drill:** write the Sprint 2 plan with `/sprint-plan`; pressure-test the goal against where-to-play and the morality questions before committing.
- **Reading / due:** [What Is Worth Building](03-what-is-worth-building.qmd). Sprint 1 review, retro, and demo due.

## Practice 12 · Mon, Nov 30 · Launch and Learn

- **Sprint / phase:** Sprint 6 in flight. Peer Product Testing assigned.
- **Emphasis:** Do not confuse a good outcome with a good decision. Read the metric as evidence, not a verdict.
- **Scripture:** John 8:32, "and ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free." A metric is a way to know the truth about your product.
- **Quote:** "The quality of your decisions and the quality of your results are not perfectly correlated." (on *resulting*, after Annie Duke)
- **Coach's story:** McKinsey, analytics and performance management. The strongest single map in the catalog: the metrics hierarchy, leading vs lagging.
- **Season ritual:** huddle. **Demo round** (second). Preview Peer Product Testing: you will be a real test user for a classmate's product.
- **Film:** a dashboard full of vanity metrics next to one leading indicator that actually predicted retention.
- **Install:** the metrics hierarchy, leading vs lagging, vanity vs actionable, AARRR, analytics architecture, event tracking.
- **Drill:** instrument three key events and query them.
- **Reading / due:** [Measuring What Matters](14-measuring-what-matters.qmd).
